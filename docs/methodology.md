# Methodology

Source of record: <https://justforauto.com/methodology/>

## What is counted

A company record counts when it is **published**, has a **verified website**, a **record-depth score ≥ 40**, and either an **English description** or a **depth ≥ 70**.

## Record depth (0–100)

| Field present | Points |
|---|---|
| Website | 30 |
| Description | 25 |
| City | 15 |
| Phone | 10 |
| Email | 10 |
| Profile link | 5 |
| Social link | 5 |

Depth measures **how complete a record is**, not how good a company is.

## Sources

Exhibitor directories (Automechanika Shanghai/Frankfurt/Dubai, EICMA, CIMAMotor), the Messe Frankfurt market directory, OpenStreetMap business listings, Wikidata companies, and curated brand-owner mapping.

Each record carries its source label; an exhibitor record states the edition it came from.

## Known limitations

- Chinese-language company names carry a **pinyin transliteration**, not an official English name. The registered Chinese name is kept on the record.
- The brand-owner mapping is **compiled from public knowledge**, not a trademark registry. Ownership changes with divestments.
- Descriptions exist in one language only. Chinese-only descriptions are not machine-translated on English pages.
- Some brand sites block automated checks; a 403 is recorded as `blocked`, never as dead.
- Counts move as records are added or re-scored. Every CSV ships with an `as_of` column.
